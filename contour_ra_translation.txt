Let \(I,J\in\{1,2\}\) label the two full contours, and define on each contour
\[
\phi_r^{(I)}=\frac12\bigl(\phi_1^{(I)}+\phi_2^{(I)}\bigr),\qquad
\phi_a^{(I)}=\phi_1^{(I)}-\phi_2^{(I)}.
\]

Also define
\[
G^>(t,x)=\langle \phi(t,x)\phi(0)\rangle,\qquad
G^<(t,x)=\langle \phi(0)\phi(t,x)\rangle,
\]
and
\[
G_R(t,x)=\theta(t)\bigl(G^>(t,x)-G^<(t,x)\bigr),\qquad
G_A(t,x)=-\theta(-t)\bigl(G^>(t,x)-G^<(t,x)\bigr).
\]

Assuming contour \((2)\) is later than contour \((1)\), the \(ra\)-basis two-point functions are:

## Diagonal contour blocks
\[
G^{(11)}_{ra}(t,x)=G^{(22)}_{ra}(t,x)=
\begin{pmatrix}
\frac12\bigl(G^>(t,x)+G^<(t,x)\bigr) & G_R(t,x) \\
G_A(t,x) & 0
\end{pmatrix},
\]
where rows/columns are ordered as \((r,a)\).

Equivalently,
\[
\langle \phi_r^{(I)}(t,x)\phi_r^{(I)}(0)\rangle
=\frac12\bigl(G^>(t,x)+G^<(t,x)\bigr),
\]
\[
\langle \phi_r^{(I)}(t,x)\phi_a^{(I)}(0)\rangle
=G_R(t,x),
\]
\[
\langle \phi_a^{(I)}(t,x)\phi_r^{(I)}(0)\rangle
=G_A(t,x),
\]
\[
\langle \phi_a^{(I)}(t,x)\phi_a^{(I)}(0)\rangle
=0,
\qquad I=1,2.
\]

## Off-diagonal contour blocks
\[
G^{(12)}_{ra}(t,x)=
\begin{pmatrix}
G^<(t,x) & 0 \\
0 & 0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
G^{(21)}_{ra}(t,x)=
\begin{pmatrix}
G^>(t,x) & 0 \\
0 & 0
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Equivalently,
\[
\langle \phi_r^{(1)}(t,x)\phi_r^{(2)}(0)\rangle = G^<(t,x),
\qquad
\langle \phi_r^{(2)}(t,x)\phi_r^{(1)}(0)\rangle = G^>(t,x),
\]
and all other mixed-contour correlators vanish:
\[
\langle \phi_r^{(1)}(t,x)\phi_a^{(2)}(0)\rangle = 0,
\qquad
\langle \phi_a^{(1)}(t,x)\phi_r^{(2)}(0)\rangle = 0,
\qquad
\langle \phi_a^{(1)}(t,x)\phi_a^{(2)}(0)\rangle = 0,
\]
\[
\langle \phi_r^{(2)}(t,x)\phi_a^{(1)}(0)\rangle = 0,
\qquad
\langle \phi_a^{(2)}(t,x)\phi_r^{(1)}(0)\rangle = 0,
\qquad
\langle \phi_a^{(2)}(t,x)\phi_a^{(1)}(0)\rangle = 0.
\]

## Full block matrix
Thus the full \(2\times 2\) contour matrix in \(ra\) space is
\[
G^{(IJ)}_{ra}(t,x)=
\begin{pmatrix}
G^{(11)}_{ra}(t,x) & G^{(12)}_{ra}(t,x) \\
G^{(21)}_{ra}(t,x) & G^{(22)}_{ra}(t,x)
\end{pmatrix},
\]
with
\[
G^{(11)}_{ra}=G^{(22)}_{ra}=
\begin{pmatrix}
\frac12(G^>+G^<) & G_R \\
G_A & 0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
G^{(12)}_{ra}=
\begin{pmatrix}
G^< & 0 \\
0 & 0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
G^{(21)}_{ra}=
\begin{pmatrix}
G^> & 0 \\
0 & 0
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

If instead contour \((1)\) is later than contour \((2)\), swap \(G^<\leftrightarrow G^>\) in the off-diagonal blocks.
